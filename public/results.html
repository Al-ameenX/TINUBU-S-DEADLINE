EDITORIAL DESK

Evidence before endorsement.

Administrators upload JSON result files. Administrators and reviewers record verification decisions against source documents. Replacing a result resets it to pending review.

Upload LGA results

Use exact state and LGA names from the directory. Supply all party vote totals and a public HTTPS link to the result sheet. Files are validated before saving; existing LGA records are replaced.

To review a result, open its LGA above. Pending and disputed results remain labeled in the public explorer.

HOW TO READ THESE RESULTS

Evidence, with a clear status.

Pending review means figures have been uploaded. Verified means an editor checked the evidence. Disputed means an editor flagged a concern. Review notes and timestamps appear with each result. No results are inferred or simulated.

Analytics you can check.

Vote share uses total valid party votes. Turnout uses total ballots cast divided by registered voters. Accreditation is shown separately. The winning margin is the difference between the two highest party totals. These are LGA figures, not a national election declaration.
